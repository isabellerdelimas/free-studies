    Help with conclusion questions 13–19

    Compare the motion arrows for Q13. Compare the old and young ocean experiments for Q14–15. Use continental collision for Q16 and folding in Q18. For Q17, compare spreading (new crust), subduction (recycling), and transform motion (neither creation nor destruction).

    For Q19, the pulling-apart process described is divergent motion. Faulting also occurs at convergent and transform boundaries; a fault is a fracture with movement, not necessarily an open gap.
